## Distribution Notes

- Third-party source and license files are not rewritten by AtomGradient.
- Apache-2.0 dependencies with `NOTICE.txt` files must preserve those notices
  in any redistributed binary or source package where the dependency is
  included.
- The local `.build/checkouts/` paths above are audit references. They are not
  committed source paths and may be regenerated by SwiftPM.
